# Structure profile: James Scott Bell (LOCK + two doorways + mirror moment)

The default profile. Drawn from James Scott Bell's craft method, principally
*Plot & Structure* and *Write Your Novel from the Middle*. This file encodes his
method and vocabulary for the skill to use; it does not reproduce his text. Cite
Bell when the bible references these concepts.

Bell is the default because the method is simple, actionable, and genre-agnostic,
which suits a game that has to hold structure across many paths.

## The method in brief

- **LOCK.** A **Lead** worth following, a clear **Objective** they want, a
  **Confrontation** strong enough that the outcome is in doubt, and a **Knockout**
  ending that resolves the Objective with force rather than fizzle.
- **Disturbance and care.** Open by disturbing the Lead's ordinary world and
  giving the audience a reason to care before the plot accelerates.
- **Two doorways of no return.** Two one-way thresholds divide the three acts. The
  **first doorway** pushes the Lead into the central conflict so they cannot go
  back; it ends Act 1. The **second doorway** forces the final push toward
  resolution; it ends Act 2.
- **The mirror moment.** At the true midpoint, the Lead is forced to confront who
  they are or what they have become. It is the thematic heart and the pivot the
  whole arc turns on. This is the question grilled in narrative-tree node 2.

## Spine ordering

Grill the structural beats of the main quest in this order:

1. **Disturbance** — what breaks the ordinary world. Depends on premise and Lead.
2. **Care** — why the audience is invested before the first doorway.
3. **First doorway** — the irreversible commitment into the conflict. Depends on
   the Objective and the opposition.
4. **Midpoint mirror moment** — the Lead confronts who they are; the theme bites.
   Depends on the theme and the Objective.
5. **Second doorway** — the final irreversible push toward resolution.
6. **Knockout** — the decisive resolution of the Objective.

## Required beats

The spine must contain all of these, numbered in this order in the bible:

- disturbance, care, first doorway, mirror moment, second doorway, knockout.

## Structural checklist (prose sanity pass)

Check the spine against these before writing the bible:

- All six beats are present and in order across the three acts.
- The mirror moment exists and is tied to the theme. Its absence is the most
  common and most damaging gap; flag it loudly.
- The knockout resolves the stated Objective, not an unrelated beat.
- Both doorways are genuinely one-way; the bible should say why return is
  impossible after each.

## Branching note

Two beats are load-bearing for the whole structure: the mirror moment and the
knockout. Keep them on the critical path and do not let a player choice route
around them, or the story loses its thematic pivot and its payoff. Forks are
safest between structural beats, where divergent paths can reconverge before the
next load-bearing beat.
